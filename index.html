I'm a fourth-year Ph. D. student in Niulab, advised by Prof. Sheng Zhou and Prof. Zhisheng Niu. I received my B.E. degree from the Department of Electronic Engineering, Tsinghua University in 2023. I'm interested in AI infrastructure, and my studies focus on green edge intelligence, inference optimization from an integrated memory-computation-communication perspective, and energy-efficient DNN/VLM/VLA inference at the edge.

Robust DNN Partitioning and Resource Allocation Under Uncertain Inference Time

Zhaojun Nan, Yunchu Han, Sheng Zhou, Zhisheng Niu

We consider edge-device collaborative inference under uncertain inference time, and the proposed robust optimization scheme only uses the mean and variance information to obtain the partition point decision and resource allocation while meeting the probabilistic deadline constraint.

CPU-Utilization-Aware Scheduling for In-Vehicle Distributed Computing

Jintao Yan, Yunchu Han, Zhaojun Nan, Sheng Zhou

We distribute computing tasks between an onboard computer and devices inside the vehicle with the proposed CPU-utilization-aware scheduling policy based on the queueing theory, reducing response times in both simulations and testbed experiments.
